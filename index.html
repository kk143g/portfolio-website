About Me

iOS & Android engineering, cross-platform architecture, and AI-augmented delivery.

I'm a Senior iOS & Android Engineer and Mobile Architect based in Lahore, Pakistan, with over a decade covering the full mobile domain — native iOS and Android, plus cross-platform delivery with React Native, Flutter, and Kotlin Multiplatform. I've led architecture on apps serving millions of users across FinTech, HealthTech, and on-demand platforms, mentored engineering teams, and consistently moved the needle on crash rates, release velocity, and App Store ratings.

On iOS that's Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit, and Combine; on Android, Kotlin and Java with Jetpack Compose; cross-platform work spans React Native (JavaScript/TypeScript) and Flutter (Dart), often sharing business logic through Kotlin Multiplatform. More recently I've folded agentic AI tools — Claude, Cursor, and ChatGPT — into that workflow to speed up delivery without cutting corners on architecture or code quality.
